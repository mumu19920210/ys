The useful parts of a browser, thoughtfully brought together so your next visit starts right where you want.

Keep pages together

Save a set of related tabs as a page group, then reopen and reorder them whenever you need them.

Find your way back

Organize bookmarks into folders and search bookmarks, saved groups, and history from your library.

Make room to read

Open supported articles in Reader, adjust the reading view, and save your place in normal browsing when history is enabled.

Your browser library stays on your iPhone.

Tabs, bookmarks, groups, and history are stored on your device and aren’t synced to a developer server or iCloud. The app uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for usage insights and diagnostics. Private browsing is temporary, but does not make you anonymous online.
