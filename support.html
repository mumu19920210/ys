Private Browsing

Private tabs use non-persistent website storage. Browsing history and search history from private tabs are not saved after those tabs are closed.

Browsing Data

History, bookmarks, shortcuts, settings, and website data are stored locally on your device. You can remove browsing data from the App’s settings.

Website Loading Issues

Check your internet connection, reload the page, or try opening the website in a new tab. Some websites may be unavailable because of compatibility, network, or regional restrictions.
