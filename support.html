Using the Browser

Enter a web address or search term in the bottom address bar and tap Go. Addresses without a scheme use HTTPS by default. Google is the default search engine for new installations; you can choose Bing or DuckDuckGo in Settings. Updating the App keeps your saved search engine preference. Back moves through page history and then returns to the start page. Use the address bar to reload and the page menu to copy or share a link.

QR Codes

Choose Scan QR code from More actions to use the camera or choose an image. Recognized content is submitted immediately: web addresses open, ordinary text is searched, and unsupported link types are rejected. Check the source of a QR code before scanning it. If camera access is denied, you can change the permission in the iOS Settings app or select an existing QR image instead.

Tabs, Bookmarks, and History

Tap the tab count to switch or add tabs. Touch and hold a tab, or swipe right, to assign it to a group; use the group menu to save its pages. Restore saved groups from Library or the start page. Normal tabs restore after relaunch, and recently closed normal tabs can be reopened from Tabs. In Library, manage bookmarks, folders, saved groups, and history.

Private Browsing

Private tabs use a separate, temporary website store and are not added to browsing history or restored after relaunch. You cannot save bookmarks from private browsing. Ending the private session clears its tabs and temporary website store. Private browsing does not make you anonymous to websites or network providers.

To end the session, tap End in the private-browsing banner or choose End private session in Tabs. Switching back to normal browsing alone does not close private tabs or clear their temporary data.

Browsing Data

Normal tabs, bookmarks, saved groups, history, and settings are stored locally. WebKit also stores cookies, cache, and other website data. The App does not sync your browsing library to other devices.

Open Settings → Clear browsing data to select categories: history, cookies and website data, cache, or normal tabs. This applies to normal browsing; clear private data by ending the private session. Clearing either history or normal tabs also removes the recently closed tabs list; those tabs can no longer be reopened from that list. Clearing cookies and website data may sign you out of websites. Bookmarks and saved page groups are kept and can be deleted separately in Library.

History keeps up to 500 entries, and the recently closed tabs list keeps up to 20 entries. Older entries are removed as new ones exceed these limits. Turning off Record normal browsing history in Settings stops new history entries but does not erase existing ones. Uninstalling the App removes its local browsing library; it is excluded from device backups, so do not use reinstallation as a routine troubleshooting step.

Browser Lock

Enable Lock browser in Settings to protect access with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. If authentication fails, try again using an available device authentication method. The developer cannot unlock the App remotely. The lock preference may remain in the device Keychain after reinstalling; turn off Lock browser before uninstalling if you want to reset that preference.

Page Translation

Open a webpage, then choose More actions → Translate page and select a language. To undo the translation, choose Show original page in the same submenu. If translation fails, check that the selected language is supported and try again. The feature translates webpage text; text embedded in images may remain unchanged.

Website Loading Issues

Check your connection and address, then reload or try the page in a new tab. Insecure HTTP, invalid certificates, or website compatibility can prevent loading. The App does not bypass certificate checks. Inactive pages may reload after a memory warning.

Blocked Websites and Searches

The App automatically blocks a limited set of explicit search terms and known adult websites, and applies safe-search settings to supported search providers. These rules may explain why a page or search does not open. They cannot identify every unsafe page, image, or video. If you believe a website is blocked incorrectly, contact support with its address only if it is safe to share.

Website Features

WebKit supports file uploads, standard website dialogs, sharing, and new windows. A website can receive a file you choose or capture for upload. Live website camera and microphone access, motion sensors, and downloads are not supported.

Third-Party Websites

Vido Browser provides web access. The developer does not own, operate, endorse, or control third-party websites or their content. Review a website’s own terms and privacy policy before providing personal information.
