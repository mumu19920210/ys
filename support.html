Using the Browser

Enter a web address or search term in the address bar. You can open multiple tabs, navigate backward or forward, reload pages, save bookmarks, and copy page links.

Private Browsing

Private tabs use a separate, temporary website store and are not added to browsing history or restored after relaunch. You cannot save bookmarks from private browsing. Ending the private session clears its tabs and temporary website store. Private browsing does not make you anonymous to websites or network providers.

Browsing Data

Normal tabs, bookmarks, saved groups, history, and settings are stored locally. WebKit also stores cookies and cache. You can delete bookmarks, history, saved groups, and selected website data in the App. The Browser Lock preference may remain in the device Keychain after reinstalling.

Website Loading Issues

Check your internet connection, reload the page, or try opening the website in a new tab. Some websites may be unavailable because of compatibility, network, or regional restrictions.

Third-Party Websites

Vido Browser provides web access. The developer does not own, operate, endorse, or control third-party websites or their content. Review a website’s own terms and privacy policy before providing personal information.
