Using the Browser

Enter a web address or search term in the address bar. You can open multiple tabs, navigate backward or forward, reload pages, save bookmarks, and copy page links.

Private Browsing

Private tabs use non-persistent website storage, and pages visited in private tabs are not added to the App’s browsing history. Bookmarks that you intentionally add are saved until you delete them. Private Browsing does not make you anonymous to websites, search providers, internet service providers, or network administrators.

Browsing Data

Bookmarks, browsing history, and browser settings are stored locally on your device. Website data such as cookies and cache may also be stored by WebKit. You can delete history, bookmarks, and website data from within the App. Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored data.

Website Loading Issues

Check your internet connection, reload the page, or try opening the website in a new tab. If a page requires JavaScript or opens a new window, review the JavaScript and pop-up settings in the App. Some websites may be unavailable because of compatibility, network, or regional restrictions.

Third-Party Websites

VidPro Browser provides unrestricted access to the web. The developer does not own, operate, endorse, or control third-party websites or their content. Please review a website’s own terms and privacy policy before providing personal information.
