Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VidPro Browser (the “App”) handles information when you use the App.

Information Collected by the Developer

The App does not require an account. The developer does not receive or store your browsing history, search history, bookmarks, cookies, or the content of pages you visit through the App. The App does not use this information for advertising or tracking.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores bookmarks, browsing history, and browser preferences locally on your device. Apple’s WebKit framework may also store cookies, cache, login sessions, and other website data required by websites you visit. The App does not synchronize this data with the developer’s servers or iCloud.

Private Browsing

Private tabs use WebKit’s non-persistent website data store, and pages visited in private tabs are not added to the App’s browsing history. A bookmark that you intentionally create is stored locally even when it is created from a private tab. Private Browsing does not make you anonymous and does not prevent websites, search providers, internet service providers, or network administrators from receiving information necessary to provide their services.

Search Providers and Third-Party Websites

When you perform a search, your query is sent to the search provider you select in the App: Google, Bing, Baidu, or DuckDuckGo. When you visit a website, that website may receive your IP address, browser and device information, cookies, search terms, information you submit, and other data needed to deliver its services. These third parties process information under their own privacy policies and terms. The developer does not control their content or privacy practices.

Permissions

The current version of the App does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, or advertising identifier. A website you visit may request browser-based access to device features; any such request is initiated by that website and is subject to iOS permission controls.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

You can delete individual bookmarks, clear browsing history, and clear WebKit website data from the App. You can also use Private Browsing to avoid saving visited pages to the App’s history. Uninstalling the App removes data stored locally by the App.

Children’s Privacy

The App is a general-purpose web browser and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. Because the App provides unrestricted web access, parents and guardians should supervise a child’s use of the App and third-party websites.

Data Security and Retention

Data managed directly by the App remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Website data is managed by WebKit and can be cleared from the App. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and third-party websites are responsible for the security of information they receive.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, legal requirements, or our data practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
