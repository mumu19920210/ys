Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Luma Browser (the “App”) handles information.

Information We Collect

The developer does not collect personal information through the App. The App does not include user accounts, advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting services.

Data Stored on Your Device

Browsing history, search history, bookmarks, shortcuts, browser settings, cookies, cache, and website data may be stored locally on your device to provide browser features. This data is not sent to the developer or synchronized with iCloud.

Private Browsing

Private tabs use non-persistent website storage. The App does not save browsing history, search history, or bookmarks created in private tabs. Private Browsing does not prevent websites, search providers, or network providers from receiving information needed to load their services.

Third-Party Services

Search queries are sent to Google Search. Websites you visit may collect information under their own privacy policies. The developer does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

Your Choices

You can delete history and bookmarks in the App and clear cookies, cache, and website data from Settings. You can also remove locally stored App data by uninstalling the App.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
