Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026 · Last updated: September 17, 2026

This policy explains how Vido Browser (the “App”) handles information. The App does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking, or analytics services.

Information Collected by the Developer

The developer does not receive your browsing history, bookmarks, saved pages, cookies, or the content of websites you visit through the App. The App does not send these records to a developer server.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores normal tabs, recently closed tabs, bookmarks, saved page groups, browser preferences, and up to 500 history entries on your device. This App-managed database is excluded from device backups. WebKit may separately store cookies, cache, login sessions, and other website data. The App does not sync its browsing library to a developer server or iCloud.

Private Browsing

Private tabs use a separate, temporary WebKit data store. They are not saved for restoration or added to browsing history, and the App does not allow bookmarks to be saved from private browsing. Ending the private session clears its tabs and temporary website store. Private browsing does not make you anonymous to websites, search providers, internet service providers, or network administrators.

Search Providers and Third-Party Websites

Search queries go to your selected provider: Bing (the default), DuckDuckGo, or Google. Scanned content is submitted immediately as a web address or search; unsupported link types are rejected. Websites you open may receive your IP address, browser information, cookies, and information you submit. These providers and websites handle data under their own policies; the developer does not control their practices.

Content Safety

The App blocks a limited set of explicit search terms and known adult websites on your device. Searches with supported providers use their safe-search settings. These measures do not inspect all website text, images, or videos and cannot guarantee that unsafe content is hidden.

Privacy Policy Hosting

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open it, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes. GitHub handles this information under its Privacy Statement.

Page Translation

If you choose to translate a webpage, the App uses Apple's Translation framework to process the page text on your device. Apple may collect API usage and performance information, including the App's bundle ID and the source and target languages, but not the original or translated text. The developer does not receive the page text through this feature.

Permissions

The App requests camera access when you choose to scan a QR code. You can also select a QR image using the system photo picker. QR images and camera frames are processed on your device and are not uploaded by the scanner. Choosing to capture or upload media for a website may prompt iOS for camera, microphone, or photo access; the selected file is sent to that website. Live website camera, microphone, motion, and orientation access is denied by the App. The App does not automatically read your clipboard.

Browser Lock

If you enable Browser Lock, the App uses iOS Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. The App does not receive biometric data. Its lock preference is stored in the device Keychain and may remain after reinstalling; turn off Browser Lock before uninstalling if you want to reset that preference. The App covers its content in the app switcher.

Your Choices and Data Deletion

You can delete bookmarks, history entries, and saved groups in Library. Settings lets you clear history, cookies and website data, cache, and normal tabs separately. Turning off history recording stops new entries but does not erase existing ones. Clearing website data may sign you out of websites. Uninstalling the App removes its local database and website data; the Keychain lock preference may remain.

Children’s Privacy

The App is a general-purpose browser and is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. Parents and guardians should supervise a child’s use of third-party websites.

Data Security and Retention

App-managed browsing data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. WebKit manages website data separately. The local database uses iOS file protection, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Third-party websites are responsible for information they receive.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, legal requirements, or our data practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new revision date.
