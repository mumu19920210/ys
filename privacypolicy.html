2. Privacy Policy

2.1 Data the Developer Does Not Collect

The current version does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, or third-party crash-reporting services, and it does not create user accounts. The developer does not collect your browsing history, bookmarks, search history, search queries, shortcuts, or browser settings through developer-operated servers.

2.2 Data Stored on Your Device

To provide browser functionality, the following data is stored on your device:

  • Browsing history, including visited URLs, page titles, and visit times;
  • Search history, including search queries, corresponding search URLs, and search times;
  • Bookmarks and custom shortcuts;
  • Settings such as JavaScript, pop-up blocking, and desktop website mode; and
  • Website data generated during standard browsing, including cookies, cache, and local website storage.

This App data is not synchronized with iCloud. You can delete bookmarks, browsing history, and search history within the App. You can also clear cookies, cache, and local website storage from Settings. Uninstalling the App will generally remove its locally stored data.

2.3 Private Browsing

Private tabs use a non-persistent website data store. The App does not add visits, searches, or bookmarks from Private Browsing to the local records described above. Private Browsing does not make you anonymous online: websites, search providers, internet service providers, or network administrators may still observe relevant network activity.

2.4 Search and Third-Party Websites

When your input is not a web address, the App sends the search query to Google Search. When you visit a website, that website or its service providers may process your IP address, request information, cookies, and data collected through website scripts under their own privacy policies. The App does not control third-party data practices.

2.5 Web Media Detection and Playback

To identify playable media on the current page, the App examines publicly available media elements, media URLs, and related page information on your device. Media you select may be passed to the system media player. This process does not upload your browsing content to developer-operated servers, but the media provider receives the network requests required for playback.

2.6 System Logs

The App may record media detection stages, source types, website domains, and candidate counts in device system logs for local debugging and troubleshooting. These logs are not actively sent to developer-operated servers. If you choose to send diagnostic information to us, review it first to ensure it does not contain information you do not wish to share.

2.7 System Features and Permissions

When you use the system share sheet, your selected link is handled by the system or the third-party service you choose. The current version does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, tracking, or notifications.

2.8 Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect children’s personal information. Children should use browsing features with guidance from a parent or guardian and should avoid submitting personal information to third-party websites.
